          <div class="recipe-detail-info">
            <span class="tagline">Chicken Recipe</span>
            <h2>Garlic Butter Chicken</h2>
            <p style="color: #b0b0b0; margin-bottom: 20px;">Juicy chicken breasts seared to golden perfection and smothered in a rich garlic butter sauce. This 30-minute meal is a weeknight hero.</p>
            <div class="meta-row">
              <div class="meta-item"><span class="meta-label">Prep</span><span class="meta-value">10 min</span></div>
              <div class="meta-item"><span class="meta-label">Cook</span><span class="meta-value">20 min</span></div>
              <div class="meta-item"><span class="meta-label">Total</span><span class="meta-value">30 min</span></div>
              <div class="meta-item"><span class="meta-label">Serves</span><span class="meta-value">4</span></div>
            </div>
            <div class="pinterest-share">
              <a href="#" class="btn-small">📌 Save to Pinterest</a>
              <a href="#" class="btn-outline" style="padding: 9px 18px; font-size:0.85rem;">Print Recipe</a>
            </div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="recipe-detail-content">
          <div class="ingredients">
            <h3 style="font-size:1.8rem;">Ingredients</h3>
            <ul>
              <li>4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts</li>
              <li>4 tbsp unsalted butter, divided</li>
              <li>6 cloves garlic, minced</li>
              <li>1 tsp salt</li>
              <li>½ tsp black pepper</li>
              <li>1 tsp paprika</li>
              <li>½ cup chicken broth</li>
              <li>1 tbsp fresh lemon juice</li>
              <li>2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped</li>
            </ul>
          </div>
          <div class="steps">
            <h3 style="font-size:1.8rem;">Instructions</h3>
            <ol>
              <li>Season chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and paprika on both sides.</li>
              <li>Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook 6-7 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Remove and set aside.</li>
              <li>In the same skillet, reduce heat to medium. Add remaining butter and garlic. Sauté for 30 seconds until fragrant.</li>
              <li>Pour in chicken broth and lemon juice. Scrape up any browned bits. Simmer for 2-3 minutes.</li>
              <li>Return chicken to the pan, spoon sauce over. Garnish with parsley and serve.</li>
            </ol>
            <p style="margin-top: 20px; color: var(--gold); font-weight: 600;">💡 Tip: Pair with crusty bread to soak up the garlic butter sauce.</p>
